# Support

## Get help

- **Bugs and questions:** open a
  [GitHub issue](https://github.com/lawzava/megapowers/issues). Include the
  megapowers version, the agent product and its version, and the steps to
  reproduce.
- **Installation and diagnosis:** read [docs/install.md](./docs/install.md).
- **Security reports:** follow [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md). Do not report a
  vulnerability in a public issue.

## Response

Support is best effort. There is no response-time commitment.
